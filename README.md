# Plant Disease Detection: Yield Loss Estimation

## Overview

This seminar project explores the use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning for plant disease detection and estimation of crop yield loss.

The proposed system combines plant-image analysis with agricultural and environmental parameters to identify diseases, analyze disease severity, predict crop yield, and estimate potential yield loss.

## Objectives

- Detect plant diseases using AI and deep learning techniques.
- Analyze disease severity and its potential impact on crop yield.
- Use agricultural and environmental parameters for yield prediction.
- Estimate yield loss by comparing predicted yield with expected optimal yield.
- Study and compare different AI/ML approaches for agricultural applications.

## Proposed Methodology

1. **Data Acquisition** – Collect plant images and relevant agricultural/environmental data.
2. **Preprocessing** – Clean and normalize data and preprocess plant images.
3. **Feature Extraction** – Extract visual and agricultural features such as disease characteristics, NDVI, rainfall, temperature, humidity, soil moisture, nutrients, and pH.
4. **Disease Detection & Severity Analysis** – Analyze plant images using deep learning approaches.
5. **Yield Prediction** – Apply machine-learning/regression approaches to estimate crop yield.
6. **Yield Loss Estimation** – Compare predicted yield with expected optimal yield to estimate potential loss.
7. **Result Display** – Present disease information, predicted yield, and estimated yield loss.
